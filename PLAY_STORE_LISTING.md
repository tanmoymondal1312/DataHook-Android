## Full description (4000 char limit)

```
Turn any website or app into a data source — without writing a backend.

DataHook gives you a ready-made API endpoint in seconds. Define the fields you want, copy the URL and key, and start receiving data. Every submission lands on your phone as a push notification.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Create an endpoint and define its fields — text, email, number, phone, date, yes/no or image.
2. Copy your ingest URL and secret API key, along with a ready-made JavaScript, cURL or HTML form snippet.
3. Paste it into your site or app. Data starts arriving, and you get notified instantly.


WHAT YOU GET

• Instant push notifications — an alert for every new submission, showing the fields you choose
• Customizable alerts — set your own title, subtitle, picture and logo for each endpoint
• Typed fields — text, email, number, phone, date, yes/no and image, each required or optional
• Copy-paste snippets — JavaScript fetch, cURL and a plain HTML form, generated for your endpoint
• Unified feed — every submission across all your endpoints in a single timeline
• Search — find any submission by its content
• Analytics — totals for today and the last 7 days, plus a 30-day activity chart per endpoint
• Export — download your data as CSV or JSON whenever you want
• Key rotation — regenerate a leaked API key without recreating the endpoint
• Tap to act — phone numbers, emails and links in a submission open straight in the right app
• Sign in with Google, or with email and password


BUILT FOR

• Developers who need a contact form backend without standing up a server
• Freelancers collecting leads from client landing pages
• Anyone wiring up a webhook receiver who wants alerts on their phone
• Testing and debugging requests from scripts, IoT devices and automations


YOUR DATA

DataHook contains no ads, no analytics SDKs and no trackers of any kind.

Your submissions belong to you. Export them at any time, and delete your account along with all of its data from inside the app — Settings, then Delete account. All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.


REQUIREMENTS

A DataHook account and an internet connection. Notification permission is optional: the app works without it, you just won't receive alerts.

Privacy policy: https://datahook.mediaghor.com/privacy/
```

~2,150 characters — comfortably inside the limit.

## Notes

- Play renders this as plain text. Blank lines and `•` bullets are what give it
  structure; markdown syntax such as `**bold**` will show up literally.
- Play's metadata policy bars ranking claims ("#1", "best"), testimonials,
  competitor names and emoji in the title. None are used above.
- Bengali localization: Play Console → Store listing → Manage translations. The
  English listing serves every locale until you add one; consider a Bengali
  listing if Bangladesh is a target market.
